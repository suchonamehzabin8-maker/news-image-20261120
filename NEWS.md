# News used — Friday, 20 November 2026

All stories below happened, or were first reported, on 20 November 2026.
Each was cross-checked in at least two sources. Where an event itself was
on Thursday 19 November, that is noted — the competition counts events
first reported on the day too. No story is presented as fact beyond what
the sources said; a subpoena, for example, is an investigation, not a
finding of wrongdoing.

## Lead — Asian markets plunge

- Asian equities fell sharply on Friday 20 November. Taiwan's Taiex sank
  6.75% on the day (about 6% in other reports) and more than 17% on the
  week — its worst week since 1990/1991. South Korea's Kospi fell 5.7%,
  Japan's Nikkei 4.8%. MSCI Asia-Pacific ex-Japan fell 3.2% in a day.
  Sources: Morningstar / Dow Jones, 20 Nov 2026
  ("Asian Equities Tumble as AI Stock Selloff Deepens");
  Reuters via WDSM/WTBX, 20 Nov 2026 ("Asian shares head for worst week
  since 2020 as AI rout deepens").
- The sell-off was driven by doubts over AI valuations and returns on
  heavy AI spending. Anthropic's record $81bn IPO — the first frontier AI
  company to go public, on 19 November — failed to restore confidence;
  Nvidia's strong earnings earlier in the week also failed to do so.
  Sources: Morningstar, Reuters (same articles as above).

## Cards

1. **Nvidia subpoenaed (TECH).** California Attorney General Rob Bonta
   served an investigative subpoena on Nvidia on Thursday, reported
   Friday 20 November. The probe asks whether advanced chips or
   confidential information may have reached China, and whether Nvidia
   misled investors and the public about that risk. It concerns former
   CEO Jensen Huang's reported affair with a woman suspected of spying
   for China; the subpoena does not establish any wrongdoing.
   Sources: California AG press release (oag.ca.gov); AP via KCAU,
   20 Nov 2026; IANS, 20 Nov 2026.
2. **Tokyo rally (PROTEST).** More than 10,000 people gathered outside
   Japan's National Diet building in Tokyo, reported 20 November,
   protesting Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi's policies — including
   revising the pacifist Constitution and expanding military
   capabilities. Organisers put the number at about 13,000.
   Sources: IANS via The State Line / The Freedom Press, 20 Nov 2026;
   Asian Lite, 20 Nov 2026; Daily Kiran, 20 Nov 2026.
   Note: some photo agencies date the rally itself to 19 November;
   the image says only that the rally was reported on 20 November
   and uses the "more than 10,000" figure all sources agree on.
3. **COP31 (CLIMATE).** The UN climate summit in Antalya entered its
   final scheduled day, Friday 20 November, with no deal. A revised
   draft published on Thursday dropped every mention of fossil fuels
   and the $120bn-a-year adaptation finance figure Africa demanded
   (tripling from a $40bn baseline by 2035); the EU says the priority
   is delivering existing pledges.
   Sources: Magna Bureau, 20 Nov 2026 ("COP31 enters its final day
   with no deal"); Diaspora Digital Media, 20 Nov 2026; Climate
   Action Network ECO 11, 20 Nov 2026.
4. **European youth strike (EUROPE).** More than 50 youth- and
   student-led organisations called a day of action on Friday
   20 November in more than 25 countries, under the slogan "Fight for
   our future, not their wars" — for housing, education and welfare,
   against living costs and militarisation. Italy held a general
   strike the same day.
   Sources: Sweden Herald, 20 Nov 2026; DiEM25, 20 Nov 2026;
   Bangkok Post via ArticleWeBlog, 20 Nov 2026.
5. **Raoni's land (BRAZIL).** President Lula visited the ailing
   94-year-old Indigenous leader Chief Raoni Metuktire on Thursday,
   and Brazil's Justice Ministry recognised the Kapot Nhinore
   Indigenous Territory — about 362,000 hectares on the Xingu River —
   as the permanent possession of the Kayapo (Mebengokre) and Yudja
   peoples. First reported widely on 20 November.
   Sources: AP via The Reflector, 20 Nov 2026; Al Jazeera via
   234radio / QNC News, 20 Nov 2026.
6. **Riyadh tower (MIDDLE EAST).** A Houthi missile struck Riyadh's
   tallest skyscraper, the 80-storey PIF Tower (headquarters of the
   Public Investment Fund), on Thursday; windows were blown out
   across seven to eight floors, with material damage but no
   injuries reported. The King Abdullah Financial District was
   closed and evacuated. First reported on 20 November.
   Sources: Reuters via Devdiscourse, 20 Nov 2026; EA WorldView,
   20 Nov 2026; The Edge via i3investor, 20 Nov 2026.
